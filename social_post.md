AI coding agents make changes while you sleep, making it hard to prove exactly what they did and when.

[Insert 10-second video clip: Terminal running `proofwork log` alongside the dashboard updating instantly]

ProofWork forces agents to log their commits and deployments onchain, creating a permanent, tamper-evident timeline of their actions. It uses a CLI for logging and a Next.js dashboard to read the events directly from the contract.
