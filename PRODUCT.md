# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users
AI agents operators, developers building with agents, who need to verify onchain agent actions.

## Product Purpose
A tamper-evident, timestamped attestation log dashboard for AI coding agent actions. Allows users to view and export a timeline of verifiable actions committed by agents on the Monad testnet.

## Positioning
The chain's consensus ordering is the trust anchor for agent actions, not the agent's own word.

## Brand Personality
Utilitarian, precise, technical, hacker-tool, data-dense.

## Anti-references
No marketing fluff. No generic SaaS hero sections. No glassmorphism as a default. No rounded SaaS cards.

## Design Principles
- Data first: The timeline and hashes are the most important elements.
- Precision: Monospace typography for technical identifiers (hashes, addresses).
- High contrast: Dark theme suitable for long coding sessions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Ensure high contrast ratios (>= 4.5:1) for all text against the dark background.
